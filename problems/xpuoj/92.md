## 题目描述

你需要实现一个 Residual Connection 算子。给定输入矩阵 `residual`、`addend1`、`addend2`（shape 均为 $(M, N)$），你需要先对一个或两个加数张量求和，再对求和结果施加 Dropout，最后乘以 `residual_scale` 并加回 `residual`，得到输出矩阵 `y`（shape 同为 $(M, N)$）。

对于每个位置 $(i, j)$，先定义线性下标：

$$
linear\_index = i \cdot N + j
$$

若 `has_addend2 = 1`，则：

$$
sum[i, j] = addend1[i, j] + addend2[i, j]
$$

若 `has_addend2 = 0`，则：

$$
sum[i, j] = addend1[i, j]
$$

然后按以下规则计算该位置的随机值：

$$
seed31 = dropout\_seed \,\&\, 0x7FFFFFFF
$$

$$
idx31 = linear\_index \,\&\, 0x7FFFFFFF
$$

$$
state = \left(seed31 \oplus \left((idx31 \cdot 1103515245 + 12345)\,\&\,0x7FFFFFFF\right)\right)\,\&\,0x7FFFFFFF
$$

$$
random\_val = \frac{state}{2^{31}}
$$

若 `random_val >= dropout_rate`，则保留该位置并乘以缩放因子 $\frac{1}{1-dropout\_rate}$；否则该位置输出 `0`。因此最终结果满足：

$$
y[i, j] =
\begin{cases}
residual[i, j] + sum[i, j] \cdot \frac{1}{1-dropout\_rate} \cdot residual\_scale, & random\_val \ge dropout\_rate \\
residual[i, j], & random\_val < dropout\_rate
\end{cases}
$$

如何提交代码详见[评测指南](https://xpuoj.com/d/2)。

## 计算过程说明

因为 `has_addend2 = 1`，所以先逐元素求和：

$$
sum = addend1 + addend2 =
\begin{bmatrix}
1.5 & 2.0 & 2.5 \\
3.0 & 3.5 & 4.0
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Dropout 的缩放因子为：

$$
\frac{1}{1 - 0.25} = \frac{4}{3}
$$

最终保留下来的加数项会再乘上 `residual_scale = 0.5`。

对每个位置计算 `linear_index = i * N + j`，再计算 `state` 与 `random_val`：

* 位置 $(0,0)$：`linear_index=0`
  * `state=(12345 ^ ((0*1103515245+12345)&0x7FFFFFFF))&0x7FFFFFFF = 0`
  * `random_val=0/2^31=0.0 < 0.25`，丢弃
  * $y_{0,0}=residual_{0,0}=1.0$
* 位置 $(0,1)$：`linear_index=1`
  * `state=1103515295`
  * `random_val \approx 0.513864 \ge 0.25`，保留
  * $y_{0,1}=2.0 + 2.0\times\frac{4}{3}\times 0.5 = 2.0 + \frac{4}{3} = 3.333333$
* 位置 $(0,2)$：`linear_index=2`
  * `state=59571498`
  * `random_val \approx 0.027740 < 0.25`，丢弃
  * $y_{0,2}=residual_{0,2}=3.0$
* 位置 $(1,0)$：`linear_index=3`
  * `state=1163078585`
  * `random_val \approx 0.541601 \ge 0.25`，保留
  * $y_{1,0}=4.0 + 3.0\times\frac{4}{3}\times 0.5 = 4.0 + 2.0 = 6.0$
* 位置 $(1,1)$：`linear_index=4`
  * `state=119101908`
  * `random_val \approx 0.055461 < 0.25`，丢弃
  * $y_{1,1}=residual_{1,1}=5.0$
* 位置 $(1,2)$：`linear_index=5`
  * `state=1222608995`
  * `random_val \approx 0.569322 \ge 0.25`，保留
  * $y_{1,2}=6.0 + 4.0\times\frac{4}{3}\times 0.5 = 6.0 + \frac{8}{3} = 8.666667$

## PyTorch 参考实现

```python
def baseline(residual, addend1, addend2, y,
             M, N, dropout_rate, dropout_seed, residual_scale, has_addend2):
    rate32 = float(torch.tensor(float(dropout_rate), dtype=torch.float32).item())
    sum_addends = addend1.float()
    if has_addend2:
        sum_addends = sum_addends + addend2.float()

    linear = torch.arange(M * N, device=residual.device, dtype=torch.int64)
    seed31 = int(dropout_seed) & 0x7FFFFFFF
    idx31 = linear & 0x7FFFFFFF
    state = (seed31 ^ ((idx31 * 1103515245 + 12345) & 0x7FFFFFFF)) & 0x7FFFFFFF
    random_val = (state.to(torch.float64) / float(1 << 31)).view(M, N)
    keep = random_val >= rate32

    if rate32 == 0.0:
        dropped = sum_addends
    else:
        dropped = torch.where(
            keep,
            sum_addends * (1.0 / (1.0 - rate32)),
            torch.zeros_like(sum_addends),
        )

    out = residual.float() + dropped * float(residual_scale)
    y.copy_(out.to(torch.bfloat16))
```
